Target Distribution

ptg is the new target distribution, that will be sampled once the method reaches convergence:

Where the bias factor γ > 1 controls the flattening of the original probability density:

  • γ = 1 ⇒ ptg = P(ξ), the probability density remains unchanging.
  • γ → ∞ ⇒ Flat target limit, uniform distribution where all states become equiprobable.
  • 1 < γ < ∞ ⇒ More flattened distribution, lowering the barriers without completely destroying the landscape.
